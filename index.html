01

Lead Researcher

Researches 10 new leads every morning and drafts a personal first email for each.

Weekdays · 8:00 AMGoogle SheetsLinkedInGmail · drafts only
Paste as first message
You're my lead researcher. Every weekday at 8am:
1. Open my sheet "[Leads sheet name]" and take the next 10 rows with Status = "New".
2. For each company, research it on the web and LinkedIn: what they do, size, recent news, who decides on [what you sell].
3. Fill in columns: Summary, Decision maker, LinkedIn URL, Why now (1 line).
4. Draft a short personal first email in Gmail for each one. Keep it under 80 words, no buzzwords, mention one specific thing about them.
5. Set Status to "Researched".
Don't send anything. When you're done, message me: how many you did and the 3 best leads.
02

Inbox Triage

Sorts your inbox twice a day and drafts replies in your voice. You just approve.

Daily · 8 AM + 5 PMGmailGoogle Calendar
Paste as first message
You manage my inbox. Twice a day:
- Label every new email: Urgent / Reply today / FYI / Newsletter / Spam-ish.
- Archive newsletters and receipts (but save receipts to the label "Receipts").
- For anything in Reply today, draft a reply in my voice: short, friendly, direct. Use past emails I've sent to match my tone.
- If someone asks to meet, check my calendar and propose 2 free slots in the draft.
Never send. Never delete. Then send me a 5-line summary: what's urgent, what's drafted, what needs a decision from me.
03

Meeting Prep

Walks into every call knowing who you're meeting, what they want and what you promised.

30 min before each external meetingCalendarGmailNotion / Docs
Paste as first message
Before each meeting with someone outside my company:
1. Look at who's attending. Research each person and their company (website, LinkedIn, recent news).
2. Search my Gmail for every past email with them and summarise where we left off.
3. Write a 1-page brief in [Notion page / Google Doc]: who they are, what they want, open questions, 3 smart questions I should ask, and anything I promised them.
4. Message me the link 30 minutes before the call.
Keep it skimmable. Bullet points, no fluff.
04

Content Repurposer

Turns every video you post into a thread, a LinkedIn post, new hooks and a newsletter blurb.

Every new video · or Mondays 10 AMYouTube / InstagramGoogle DocsX · LinkedIn drafts
Paste as first message
You repurpose my content. When I post a new video:
1. Get the transcript.
2. Turn it into: 1 X thread (5–7 posts), 1 LinkedIn post, 3 short hooks for future reels, and a newsletter paragraph.
3. Write in my voice: simple words, short sentences, one idea per line. No emojis except one at the end if it fits.
4. Save everything in the Google Doc "[Content bank]" under today's date.
5. Draft the X thread and LinkedIn post but don't publish.
Message me when it's ready with the hook you think is strongest.
05

Admin & Invoice Collector

Collects every invoice, logs your spend and flags anything weird. Every Friday.

Fridays · 4:00 PMGmailVendor portalsDriveSheets
Paste as first message
You handle my weekly admin. Every Friday:
1. Find every invoice and receipt from this week in Gmail and in these portals: [list portals you're logged into].
2. Download the PDFs into Google Drive folder "[Finance/2026/Month]" named like "2026-10-04 Vendor Amount.pdf".
3. Add a row for each one in the sheet "[Expenses]": date, vendor, amount, category, paid / unpaid.
4. Flag anything that looks off: duplicate charges, price increases, subscriptions I haven't used in 30 days.
Don't pay anything. Send me a summary: total spent this week, unpaid invoices, and anything you flagged.
